ABOUT

Tam Son Art is founded by Master Lacquer Artist Vu Cuong. With nearly 30 years of dedication to the art of design and lacquer painting, I specialize in creating high-end, handcrafted lacquerware that bridges traditional Vietnamese heritage and modern artistic expression. My works are deeply rooted in Vietnamese culture, utilizing sophisticated materials such as natural lacquer, eggshell, mother-of-pearl, and gold leaf. Every piece is a silent conversation between the artist, the material, and timeless tradition.
