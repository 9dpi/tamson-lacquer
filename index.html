ABOUT

My practice is driven by my belief in our interconnection with our environment. Currently, my interest is in future plant life, informed by my research in plant consciousness and the ways plants communicate with each other and with us. Through large-scale, highly detailed drawings in coloured pencil and text marker, I create speculative “plants of the future.”
